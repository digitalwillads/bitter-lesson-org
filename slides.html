The lesson

Seventy years of AI. One pattern, four beats.

Researchers built their own knowledge into the machine. It helped at first. Then it flattened. Search and learning on more compute won in the end, by a lot.

What it means for us

Do not build our know-how into the machine. Build the machine that finds it.

Every rebuild takes the system offline for weeks. A loop never goes offline. It gets one line better each round.

The whole system

One loop. The playbook feeds the agents. The agents feed the playbook.

A cron starts the agents. They read the playbook, work through Barbara, and log every job. Exceptions become SOP updates, and leadership approves them in Slack.

The playbook

Plain text files. One per SOP. One per client.

The agents read them before every job. When a job falls outside them, the agent writes one line to the exceptions log. The hook turns that line into a change to the file.

The approval gate

People approve the rules, not the work.

The proposal lands in the leadership channel. Approve, and it is live in the next round. Send it back with a note, and the note goes into the next proposal.

Specialists for hard jobs

One general agent for routine work. A named specialist for each hard job.

A specialist has its own tools, its own SOPs and its own tests. Conversion tracking is the first. Ads pushes and client reports come next.

Rounds, not releases

Several rounds a day. Each round can change the playbook by one line.

A round is small: pull, work, log, propose. Nothing waits for a big release.

Where people sit

Your time goes to judgment, not to waiting.

Why it compounds

Our layer is text files and loops, not custom code.

The labs ship a better model every few months. The new model reads the same playbook on day one. The whole agency gets better the same day, for free.

What we set up

  1. Everyone works in Claude Code inside CMUX. Barbara is the hub for clients, email, meetings, tasks and Slack.
  2. One playbook folder. One MD per SOP. One MD per client. One exceptions log.
  3. The hook and the leadership channel. Exceptions in. SOP updates out. Approved in Slack. Live on the next round.
We want AI agents that can discover like we can, not which contain what we have discovered.
